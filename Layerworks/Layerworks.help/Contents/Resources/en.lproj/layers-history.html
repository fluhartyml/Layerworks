Layers, History and Undo

The inspector's Layers tab lists the layers, top first. Select a layer, then use a tool on it. Click the eye to hide or show a layer. Add Layer adds an empty one.

Check two or more layers to Merge them into one, or Link them so they move and resize together. Right-click a layer to Rename…, Duplicate, Unlink or Delete it.

History is the undo

Every change becomes a step in the History tab. Edit ▸ Undo (⌘Z) and Edit ▸ Redo (⇧⌘Z) move back and forward through it. Click a step to look at the picture as it was then. Changing something while you look back drops the newer steps, the same as Undo followed by a new change.

Right-click a step for Restore from This Step or Delete This Step. While you look at an earlier step, a Keep This Step button appears.

Keeping files small

Optimize History frees space and keeps every step. Purge History… keeps the picture as it is now and clears the older steps; it asks first. Both are in the History tab and the File menu.
