Animation, Storyboard and video

Capture frames

Pick the Camera tool (H) and turn on Animation mode. Put moving parts on their own layers. Capture Frame, move things, and capture again. Onion skin shows the last few frames faintly. Generate draws in-between frames by moving the layers, with an optional slow in and out. Play shows the frames at the chosen speed; Exit Preview (Esc) stops.

Storyboard

Choose File ▸ Storyboard… (⌥⌘B), or Storyboard… in the Camera tool. For each frame you can set how long it holds, move it up or down, or remove it. Between two frames, choose a transition (Dissolve, Fade Through Black or White, Swipe, Bars, Copy Machine, Flash, Shards or Page Curl) and its length. Add Soundtrack… picks your own sound file; it plays from the start and stops where the video ends. Play makes the video and plays it in the sheet.

A frame with no hold of its own shows at the Lightbox speed set in the Camera tool.

Export the video

Choose File ▸ Export…, pick Video (Storyboard) and a format: H.264 (.mp4), HEVC (.mp4) or ProRes 422 (.mov). Video is 30 frames per second on a white background.
