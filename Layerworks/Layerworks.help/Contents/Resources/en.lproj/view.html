Zoom and the window

View ▸ Zoom In (⌘=), Zoom Out (⌘−), Actual Size (⌘0) and Fit Width (⌘9). You can also pinch on a trackpad, or use the zoom panel under the canvas; click its level to return to 100%.

View ▸ Canvas Focus (⇧⌘F) hides everything but the canvas. Choose it again to come back.

View ▸ Show Inspector shows or hides the tool settings, Layers and History. The status line along the bottom says what each action did.
