Tools and their keys

The tool box sits under the canvas. Pick a tool, then use its settings in the inspector. Each tool also has a single key with no modifier, listed in the Tools menu. Click the canvas first so a text box doesn't have the keyboard; while you type in a text box, letters are just typing.

KeyToolWhat it does
CCanvas ToolCanvas size, resolution, presets
KPaletteThe picture's colors (8 to 24)
MMoveMove, scale, turn or crop the selected layer
SSelectionSelect an area to copy, paste or clear
QMaskKeep only what's inside a shape when you export
BPaint BucketFill an area or the whole layer
PPenDraw pixels
EEraserErase pixels, or erase one color everywhere
IEyedropperPick a color from the picture
TTextWords on their own layer, still editable; 3D letters
YSymbolAn SF Symbol on its own layer
GImageBring in a picture or a photo
AImage PlaygroundApple's Image Playground: New Layer or Filter Current Layer
LLayerTranslucent, Glow, Green Key, Gradient, Blend
RRemove BackgroundLift the subjects off their background
WMagic LassoClick an area to clear it
JAdjustLevels, Gaussian Blur, Liquify, Round Corners, Flip
HCameraCapture the picture, or capture frames for animation

Changes that redraw a layer make a new layer above it and hide the original, so you can always go back.

Image and Filter menus

Image ▸ Adjustments ▸ Levels… (⌘L), Image ▸ Flip…, Filter ▸ Gaussian Blur…, Filter ▸ Liquify… (⇧⌘X) and Filter ▸ Round Corners… open the Adjust tool on that adjustment.

Image Playground

Image Playground needs Apple Intelligence turned on in System Settings. You type the words in Apple's own Image Playground window, and Done adds the picture as a new layer.
