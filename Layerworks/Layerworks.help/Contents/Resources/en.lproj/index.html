Layerworks Help

Layerworks makes pictures in layers, with a History of every change, tools for drawing, text, symbols, photos and Apple's Image Playground, and export for print, the web, app icons and video.

  • Make, open and save a picture — New pictures stay Untitled until you save them.
  • Layers, History and Undo — Every change is a History step; Undo and Redo move through History.
  • Tools and their keys — Each tool has a one-letter key; type it while the canvas has the keyboard.
  • Zoom and the window — Zoom with the View menu; Canvas Focus gives the canvas the whole window.
  • Export, share and print — File ▸ Export… saves the picture in many formats.
  • Animation, Storyboard and video — Capture frames with the Camera tool, then time them in the Storyboard and export a video.
  • Command mode — Run a job file from Terminal or a script, with no window.
  • Accessibility — VoiceOver, Voice Control, keyboard and Reduce Motion.
